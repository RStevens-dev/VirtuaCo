Potential future sources: Google Scanned Objects (CC BY 4.0; check individual packaging rights), Amazon Berkeley Objects (review applicable license and terms), Poly Haven (CC0), Kenney (CC0), Open Food Facts (separate database and image licenses). None of these additional libraries are imported yet. Review each asset, attribution, trademarks, redistribution permission, polygon count, texture size and actual dimensions before adding it to assets.js.
